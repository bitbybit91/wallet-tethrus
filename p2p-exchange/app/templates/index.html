How It Works

For Buyers

  1. Browse sell offers or create a buy offer
  2. Initiate a trade with a seller
  3. Wait for seller to fund escrow on-chain
  4. Send fiat payment to seller
  5. Seller confirms receipt & escrow releases USDT

For Sellers

  1. Create a sell offer with your terms
  2. Wait for a buyer to initiate a trade
  3. Fund the escrow address with USDT-TRC20
  4. Wait for buyer's fiat payment
  5. Confirm fiat received to release escrow
